PocketVGM Arcade

Companion core to PocketVGM, carrying the chips that do not fit alongside
the console set: YM2151, SegaPCM, MSM6258, OKIM6295 and K053260. Between
them they cover Sharp X68000, Sega arcade boards such as OutRun, and
Konami arcade.

Music lives in the same place as for PocketVGM, Assets/pocketvgm/common,
and the two cores share it. Load a .vgm or .vgz from any of the menu
entries; the file itself says which chips it needs.

A file written for the console chips will be silent here, and the reverse
is also true. Which core a recording needs is not shown anywhere yet.

Source and releases: https://github.com/M4chanic/pocketvgm
