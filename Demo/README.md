## Chip tests

`Chip tests/` holds two synthetic VGM files written for this project (CC0),
covering the chips that no freely licensed music exists for:

| File | Chip | Variant | What to expect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scc_test.vgm` | SCC (K051649) | Console | a rising scale, A4 to A5 |
| `k053260_test.vgm` | K053260 | Arcade + X68k | five PCM notes, falling pitch |

Load them from either console Load menu (both accept `.vgm`). They are the
quickest way to check that a bitstream variant really has its chips: silence
means the chip is not in the running variant. Generated by
`scripts/demo_tunes/make_vgm_tests.py`.

The demo tunes (.nsf/.gbs/.sid) are tiny purpose-built players (6502/SM83)
assembled by the generators in `scripts/demo_tunes/`; verified through RTL
simulation (`chipbox_tb --nsffile/--gbsfile/--sidfile`).
